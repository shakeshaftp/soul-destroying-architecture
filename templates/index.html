Why it matters

Beauty is not a finishing touch. It decides what gets built at all.

Wellbeing

People do not merely prefer beautiful places, they behave differently in them. A street worth walking down is walked down. And the buildings we put up outlast almost every other decision a city makes.

Housing

A good deal of what gets called NIMBYism is a reasonable objection to ugliness. Neighbours shown something worth having are far readier to accept density — which is how young and working families get a foot on the ladder.

Environment

The greenest building is the one nobody wants to demolish. Beauty earns a building the affection that keeps it standing, maintained and adapted, instead of replaced every forty years.

The comparisons

Not old against new. Loved against unloved.

The easiest way to dismiss an argument about beauty is to call it nostalgia, so this set is chosen to make that difficult. Each pair holds two buildings of the same use at a similar scale, several of the buildings we admire here are modern, and some come from well outside the Western tradition. Every pair states why it is a fair comparison.
